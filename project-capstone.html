Capstone: smart plant watering system

A sensor node that waters a plant on its own, deciding when based on soil moisture, the environment and a prediction model in a companion app.

My role PCB, enclosure, watering detectionTools KiCad, Fusion 360Year 2025

Overview

The node collects sensor data and talks to a mobile app over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. In the app, a decision model works out the best time to water and sends the command back to the device.

What I built

  • The custom sensor and controller PCB in KiCad, from schematic through layout.
  • The enclosure in Fusion 360, housing the board and routing the wiring.
  • A detection algorithm that recognises and logs each time the plant is watered.
