Bandgap voltage reference

A reference voltage that holds steady over temperature by adding a PTAT term, which rises with temperature, to a base–emitter voltage, which falls with it. This design is in progress; results will be posted here as each stage is verified.

Process GF180MCU open PDKTools LTspiceReference Razavi ch. 12In progress

Build plan

  1. PTAT current generatorTwo bipolar branches at different current densities, so their VBE difference scales with absolute temperature.
  2. CTAT branchCharacterise VBE against temperature in the PDK to find its slope.
  3. Zero-order bandgapWeight the PTAT and CTAT terms so their temperature slopes cancel at room temperature.
  4. Curvature correctionOptional: reduce the residual second-order drift left after first-order cancellation.
  5. Temperature sweepMeasure output voltage and drift in ppm/°C from −40 to 125 °C, then across process corners.
