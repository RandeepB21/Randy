How I work

Every design starts on paper: bias currents, overdrive voltages and pole locations from Sedra/Smith and Razavi. Only then does it go into LTspice, in a fixed order: DC operating point, AC response, transient, then a comparison against the hand numbers. When they disagree, that gap is usually the most interesting part of the write-up.

Analog coursework at Waterloo covered ECE 240, 340, 380 and 444, and in ECE 432 I worked in Cadence Virtuoso and Spectre on a 22 nm PDK. Alongside the analog work I'm building RTL and SystemVerilog verification skills in a separate track.
